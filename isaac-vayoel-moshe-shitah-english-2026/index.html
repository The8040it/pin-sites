About the film

This film explains the shitah of the Satmar Rebbe, Rabbi Yoel Teitelbaum zt"l, as he sets it out in Sefer Vayoel Moshe: the Hakdama, Maamar Shalosh Shevuos, Maamar Yishuv Eretz Yisroel and Maamar Lashon HaKodesh. Every proof is given with maamar and siman; when we quote the sefer you see the actual scan of the page with the words marked, followed by the translation.

Edition: Sefer Vayoel Moshe, new edition, Hotzaas Sefarim Yerushalayim, Brooklyn 5766; the title page of the first edition (Brooklyn 5721) is reproduced there.

Bibliography

The sefer: Sefer Vayoel Moshe, by the Satmar Rebbe, Rabbi Yoel Teitelbaum zt"l, new edition, Hotzaas Sefarim Yerushalayim, Brooklyn 5766 (with the title page of the first edition, Brooklyn 5721). Scan: mysatmar.com. All quotations from the sefer are from this scan, with maamar and siman.

What is missing and what is uncertain

  • We used the Brooklyn 5766 edition. Of the first edition (5721) we have only the title page reproduced there. Page numbers are from the new edition; that is why we cite by maamar and siman.
  • The text of the sefer was read from a scan in Rashi script. Every quotation was checked on the page with a second, enlarged reading. For two quotations we could not crop a clear image of the page; they are read without a scan on screen.
  • For a small number of simanim in Maamar Yishuv Eretz Yisroel and Maamar Lashon HaKodesh, the exact point on the page where the siman begins is not fully certain.
  • In the biography, certain details are disputed (the year of birth, how he came onto the train of 1944, and when and how the sefer was written). We bring the different versions and say when something is not clear.
  • We do not have confirmed sources for the position of certain other gedolim on these matters, and we do not speak about it.
  • Matters that came later (for example 5727 and Al HaGeulah ve'al HaTemurah) are marked as later, not as part of Vayoel Moshe.
  • Authentic photographs of the Rebbe are few; we use only documented photographs, no generated images. The other pictures (seforim, batei medrash, towns) show the place or the subject, and are labeled as such.
  • This English version follows the original English script from which the Yiddish narration was translated, with the same content, chapters and pictures. Hebrew quotations are read in Ashkenazi transliteration followed by their English translation.
  • The English voice is a synthetic (computer) voice; some Hebrew words and names may not be pronounced exactly.
