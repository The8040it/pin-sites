    How this works

    Prices come from four price sites, one per area, which read each store's own online-ordering prices: KJ Grocery Prices, Boro Park Grocery Prices, Monsey Grocery Prices and Williamsburg Grocery Prices. All the credit for the price data goes to them.

    For each area you see the lowest price at any store there (the sale price when there is one), and which store has it. The 👑 marks the cheapest area.

    The scoreboard adds up the cheapest price in each area for every product sold in all four areas, leaving out pairs whose prices are too far apart to be the same item.

    Products are matched across areas by barcode. The Williamsburg site doesn't publish barcodes, so its items (and a few others) are matched by the exact product name and size, or by the shared product photo with the same size. Those prices are marked ≈; double-check the size before you rely on them.
