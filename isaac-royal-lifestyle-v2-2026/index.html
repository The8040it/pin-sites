What is real and what is fictional

The prince, his kingdom, his palaces, his household and the scenes of his daily life are fictional. Everything said about real history, real palaces, titles, protocol, households, objects, brands and places was checked against reference sources while the film was made (October 2026). Royal authority, wealth and privileges differ greatly between countries and institutions; nothing in the film claims real-world authority for the fictional prince. Opinions are labelled as opinions in the narration.
