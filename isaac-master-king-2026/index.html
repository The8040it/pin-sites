Fact, legend and imagination

Scenes of “our imagined Master King”, his palace, his kingdom, his household and the day in his life (chapter 12) are fictional reconstructions; every such picture carries the small label “Illustration · imagined scene”. Everything said about real rulers, palaces, regalia, institutions, research and history was checked against reference sources while the film was made (October 2026); the narration says when something is legend or tradition, or when historians disagree. No statistics, quotations or financial figures were invented; opinions on contested questions, such as the value of monarchy, are presented from more than one side.
