Network Setup & Configuration

Full LAN/WAN setup, managed switches, access points, structured cabling advice and network segmentation for homes and offices.

Request help845-579-2963

Overview

Slow, patchy Wi-Fi and random disconnections are some of the most frustrating day-to-day IT problems. A well-planned network fixes them: the right equipment in the right places, configured properly and secured.

We design and set up networks for homes and offices, from a single router to managed switches, multiple access points and separate networks for guests, staff and smart devices. We work with equipment from vendors such as Ubiquiti and Cisco.

What's included

  • Network assessment and Wi-Fi coverage planning
  • Router setup and secure configuration
  • Managed switches and VLANs
  • Wi-Fi access points and mesh systems for full coverage
  • Network segmentation: guest, staff and smart-device networks
  • Structured cabling advice
  • Printer, NAS and shared-device networking
  • Network documentation: what's connected, where and how

Frequently asked questions

Do I need a mesh system or access points?

For most homes, a good mesh system works well. For offices or larger spaces, wired access points usually give the best performance and reliability. We'll recommend what fits.

Why should I separate my network?

Separating guests and smart devices from the computers that hold your files limits the damage if one device is compromised.

Can you work with my internet provider's equipment?

Yes. We can configure what you have or recommend better equipment if it's holding you back.

Do you run network cables?

We provide structured cabling advice and planning. Contact us to discuss the cabling work your space needs.
