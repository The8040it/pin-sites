Cybersecurity Services

Overview

Most cyber attacks on small businesses and individuals aren't sophisticated. They rely on weak passwords, unpatched software, a convincing phishing email or a single unprotected device. The good news: most of them can be prevented with the right basics, done consistently.

We help you find the weak spots before attackers do, clean up infections, protect every device and teach your team what to look out for. Security best practice is built into everything we do.

What's included

  • Vulnerability assessments of devices, network and accounts
  • Penetration testing to see how defences hold up
  • Malware, ransomware and adware removal
  • Endpoint protection deployment and monitoring
  • Multi-factor authentication rollout
  • Password manager setup and policy
  • Encryption, for example BitLocker for Windows devices
  • Staff security awareness training, including phishing

Frequently asked questions

I think I've been hacked. What should I do first?

Disconnect the affected device from the internet, don't enter any more passwords on it, and contact us. From a different, clean device, change the password of any important account you think is affected and turn on two-factor authentication.

Is antivirus enough?

It's important but not enough on its own. Updates, strong unique passwords, multi-factor authentication, backups and alert users matter just as much.

What is penetration testing?

An authorized, controlled attempt to find and exploit weaknesses in your systems, so you can fix them before a real attacker finds them.

Do you offer security training for staff?

Yes. We offer staff security awareness training, including how to recognise phishing. Our phishing guide is a good place to start.
