IT Consulting & Strategy

Technology roadmaps, vendor evaluation, digital transformation guidance, IT budgeting and ongoing virtual CTO advisory for small businesses.

Request help845-579-2963

Overview

Technology decisions are business decisions. Which software, which equipment, what to spend this year and what can wait. Without guidance, it's easy to overspend on the wrong things and under-invest in the ones that matter, like security and backups.

We act as your technology advisor: we look at where you are, where your business is going, and build a practical, budgeted plan to get there. For growing businesses we offer ongoing virtual CTO advice.

What's included

  • IT assessment: devices, software, network, security and costs
  • Technology roadmap for the next 12–36 months
  • Vendor and software evaluation, with honest recommendations
  • IT budgeting and replacement planning
  • Digital transformation guidance: moving paper and manual processes to digital tools
  • Security and backup strategy
  • Virtual CTO advisory: ongoing, regular strategy guidance
  • Support for new offices, moves and growth

Frequently asked questions

What is a virtual CTO?

An experienced technology advisor who works with your business part-time or on an ongoing basis, giving strategic IT guidance without the cost of a full-time executive.

Do you sell hardware or software?

Our job is to recommend what you actually need, not what costs more. That's one of our core values.

Is consulting only for larger companies?

No. Small businesses often benefit most, because every technology dollar has to count.

How does a consulting engagement start?

Every engagement starts with a free assessment. Get in touch and tell us what you're trying to achieve.
