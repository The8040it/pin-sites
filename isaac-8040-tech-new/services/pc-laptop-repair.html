PC & Laptop Repair

Hardware diagnostics, component replacement, screen and keyboard repair, and full system teardowns for Dell, HP, Lenovo, Apple, ASUS and more.

Request help845-579-2963

Overview

When a computer won't start, crashes, overheats or just behaves strangely, the first job is finding out why. We start with proper hardware diagnostics, so you're paying to fix the real cause, not to swap parts until something works.

We service desktops and laptops from all major brands, including Dell, HP, Lenovo, Apple and ASUS, and we explain the problem in plain language before any work begins.

What's included

  • Full hardware diagnostics: storage, memory, power, temperatures and ports
  • Motherboard inspection and component-level troubleshooting
  • Component replacement: drives, RAM, batteries, fans and power supplies
  • Laptop screen and keyboard repair or replacement
  • Full system teardowns, internal cleaning and thermal care
  • Data-safe handling: we protect your files before any repair work
  • Operating system repair when the hardware is healthy but the software isn't
  • A clear write-up of what was wrong and what we did

Common problems we solve

Won't turn on or won't bootPower, battery, storage or startup faults traced to the actual cause.
Overheating and loud fansDust, worn fans or dried thermal paste that make a machine throttle and shut down.
Cracked screen or dead keysDisplay, hinge and keyboard problems on laptops.
Blue screens and random crashesFailing memory or drives, driver conflicts and corrupted system files.
Clicking or failing hard driveEarly signs of drive failure, caught before your data is lost.
Liquid spills and physical damageAssessment of what's recoverable and what it will take to repair.

Frequently asked questions

Which brands do you work on?

All major brands, including Dell, HP, Lenovo, Apple and ASUS. If you have something less common, ask us; most repairs follow the same principles.

Will I lose my files?

Protecting your data comes first. Where a repair could put data at risk, we'll talk about a backup before we start.

Is it worth repairing an older computer?

Often, yes. A new SSD or more RAM can transform an older machine. If a repair costs more than the computer is worth, we'll tell you honestly.

Do you offer on-site repairs?

Many issues can be handled remotely, and on-site visits can be scheduled at your convenience. Contact us to discuss your situation.
