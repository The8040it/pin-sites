Device Support

Device & Peripheral Setup

Printer installation, scanner configuration, external drives, docking stations, multi-monitor setups and KVM switch configuration.

Request help845-579-2963

Overview

New equipment should make work easier, not eat up an afternoon. We set up printers, scanners, monitors, docks and drives properly, with the right drivers, settings and connections, so everything works together from day one.

Whether it's one printer for a home office or a multi-monitor desk for a team, we get it working and show you how to use it.

What's included

  • Printer installation for USB, network and Wi-Fi printers, including shared office printers
  • Scanner and scan-to-email or scan-to-folder configuration
  • External drives, set up and formatted for your backups
  • Docking stations and USB-C or Thunderbolt hubs
  • Multi-monitor setups: arrangement, resolution and scaling
  • KVM switch configuration to control several computers from one keyboard and mouse
  • Webcams, headsets and conference equipment
  • Correct drivers and firmware updates

Frequently asked questions

Can you help me choose a printer or monitor?

Yes. We can recommend equipment that fits your needs and works with your existing setup, before you buy.

Can printers be set up remotely?

Often, yes, if the printer is already connected to your network. Physical installation and cabling need an on-site visit.

Will my old printer work with my new computer?

Usually, but not always. Some older devices no longer get driver updates. We'll check before you spend money on workarounds.

Do you set up equipment for whole offices?

Yes. We support home offices, small businesses and growing companies.
