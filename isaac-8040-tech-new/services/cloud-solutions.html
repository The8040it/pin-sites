Overview

Moving email, files and tools to the cloud can make work simpler and more flexible, as long as it's planned properly. A rushed migration means lost emails, broken permissions and confused staff.

We deploy and manage Microsoft 365 and Google Workspace, move your existing email and files across, manage setups that mix cloud and on-premises systems, and lock down cloud accounts so they're properly secured. We also work with AWS.

What's included

  • Microsoft 365 and Google Workspace setup
  • Custom-domain business email

  • Shared drives, Teams, SharePoint or Google Drive structure and permissions
  • Cloud migration strategy and planning
  • Hybrid environments that mix cloud and on-premises systems
  • Security hardening: MFA, admin roles, sharing and login policies

Frequently asked questions

Should I choose Microsoft 365 or Google Workspace?

Both are excellent. Microsoft 365 suits teams that rely on desktop Word, Excel and Outlook; Google Workspace is simple and browser-first. We'll recommend based on how you work.

Will I lose my old emails when moving?

Not with a planned migration. Existing email can be moved to the new platform.

Is the cloud secure?

The big platforms are very secure, but most breaches come from weak account settings. Multi-factor authentication and good admin practices matter most.

Is my data backed up just because it's in the cloud?

Cloud providers keep their service running, but you're responsible for protecting your data against deletion or account compromise. A separate backup is recommended.
