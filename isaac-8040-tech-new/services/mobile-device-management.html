Mobile Device Management

MDM enrollment, app distribution, remote wipe, policy enforcement and BYOD security for iOS and Android fleets.

Request help845-579-2963

Overview

Phones and tablets hold company email, files and passwords, and they're easy to lose. Mobile Device Management (MDM) lets a business keep work devices secure and consistent without touching each one by hand.

We set up and manage MDM for iOS and Android devices, from enrolling the first phone to setting policies, pushing apps and preparing for the day a device goes missing. We work with tools such as Microsoft Intune.

What's included

  • MDM platform setup, for example Microsoft Intune
  • Enrollment of company-owned iOS and Android devices
  • BYOD (bring your own device) policies that separate work and personal data
  • App distribution and updates
  • Security policies: passcodes, encryption and OS update requirements
  • Remote lock and remote wipe for lost or stolen devices
  • Email, Wi-Fi and VPN profiles pushed automatically
  • Onboarding and offboarding procedures for staff

Frequently asked questions

Does MDM let my employer see my personal photos or messages?

A properly configured BYOD setup manages only the work apps and data. Personal content stays separate. We set it up with privacy in mind and can explain exactly what is and isn't visible.

What happens if a phone is stolen?

With MDM in place, the device can be locked or wiped remotely so company data isn't exposed.

Do we need MDM for a small team?

If your team keeps work email or files on phones, MDM is worth considering even for a few devices. We'll help you decide.

Does it work with both iPhone and Android?

Yes. Modern MDM platforms manage both iOS and Android.
