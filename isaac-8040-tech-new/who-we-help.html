Who we help

IT support for every stage

We support individual users, home offices, small businesses and growing companies. Our services scale to fit your situation.

Individuals

Technology at home should be simple. We help with the everyday problems that eat up your time and the security basics that protect your family's data.

Recommended: PC & Laptop Repair, Performance Optimization, Data Backup & Recovery

How we help

  • Slow or broken laptops and desktops
  • Wi-Fi dead zones and dropping connections
  • Email, phone and account setup
  • Moving to a new computer
  • Backups for photos and documents
  • Virus removal and account security

Home offices

When your home is your office, IT problems stop your work. We set up a dependable, secure workspace so you can focus on your job.

Recommended: Device & Peripheral Setup, Firewall & VPN, Remote IT Support

How we help

  • Reliable Wi-Fi or wired connections for video calls
  • Printer, scanner, monitor and dock setup
  • Secure VPN access to your company network
  • Microsoft 365 or Google Workspace help
  • Automatic backups
  • Remote support when something breaks

Small businesses

Small teams rarely have an IT department, but they face the same threats as large companies. We become your IT team, with the support, security and advice you need.

Recommended: Network Setup & Configuration, Cybersecurity Services, Cloud Solutions

How we help

  • Office network design and setup
  • Business email and cloud migration
  • Cybersecurity basics: MFA, endpoint protection, training
  • Backups and disaster recovery planning
  • New staff onboarding and offboarding
  • Ongoing support through our Business Support plan

Growing companies

Growth brings more people, more devices and more risk. We help you plan and build infrastructure that scales, without overspending.

Recommended: IT Consulting & Strategy, Mobile Device Management, Firewall & VPN

How we help

  • Technology roadmaps and IT budgeting
  • Mobile device management for phones and tablets
  • Site-to-site VPN for multiple locations
  • Vendor evaluation and software selection
  • Security assessments and penetration testing
  • Virtual CTO advisory
